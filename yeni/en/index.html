KM 01Route

Kilometres first, then the fun.

Between the same two points it compares several options and suggests the shortest. On a multi-stop tour it works out the order of the stops, too. The rest is up to you:

  • Shortest routeThe default; distance comes first.
  • Back roads and Coast ProLeave the main road for the twisty ones.
  • EnduroLooks for gravel and dirt roads.

KM 02Tour generator

Pick a direction and a time, get a tour.

Turn the dial to the direction you want to ride and choose how many hours you have. Motoseyir builds a tour that way; see it on the map and start if you like it.

KM 03Mountain passes

1,600+ passes, on one map.

Every pass has a photo, its altitude and the months it is closed. Add the ones you like to your tour and go.

  • 2.764 mCol de l’IseranClosed Nov–May
  • 2.758 mPasso dello StelvioClosed Nov–Apr

KM 04AI CoRider
