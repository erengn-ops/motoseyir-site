KM 01Routing

Kilometres first, fun second.

It compares the alternatives between the same two points and suggests the shortest. On a multi-stop tour it works out the order of the stops, too. The rest is up to you.

  • Shortest routeThe default; it goes by kilometres.
  • Back roads and Coast ProLeaves the main road for the twisty ones.
  • EnduroLooks for gravel and unpaved roads.

KM 02Tour generator

Turn the dial to the way you want to go and choose how many hours you have. Motoseyir draws a tour in that direction; you see it on the map and start if you like it.

Privacy

No account, no unnecessary data

You do not have to give a name, an email address or a phone number to use Motoseyir. What follows is a short summary of the privacy policy; the full policy is inside the app in English, under Settings → Privacy Policy.

  • You do not need to create an account — we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • To calculate your route, your location and search queries are passed to map services only; none of them store anything.
  • Your tours, ride recordings, journal notes and photos stay only on your device — unless you share them in Community.
  • An irreversible, anonymous identity hash of your device is sent to our server to stop trial and campaign entitlements being abused; that hash does not identify you.
  • If you turn on group riding, your last location is shared only while the ride is running and is deleted when it ends. Intercom audio is never recorded, only passed to whoever is in the room at that moment.
  • No ads; no data is shared with any advertising network.
