For riders who love a corner.

Kilometres first, fun second.

It compares the alternatives between the same two points and suggests the shortest. On a multi-stop tour it works out the order of the stops, too. The rest is up to you.

  • Coast ProIn tours, it looks for roads that follow the shoreline.

  • Lane guidance

    Before a junction it shows which lane to be in on the screen and, in compatible cars on iOS 18 or later, on the car’s instrument cluster too. Lane data exists only for junctions that have it on the map.

  • Speed limits

    Where the limit is known, the speed sign stays on screen.

  • No toll roads

    One tap takes toll roads out of the route.

  • On the lock screen

    Your next turn shows on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you don’t have to unlock the phone.

Where the data comes from

  • LocationThe OpenStreetMap point that marks the pass. It sits on the road at the summit, not at an encyclopedia’s rough coordinate.
  • AltitudeMost passes use the altitude on their OpenStreetMap entry, with terrain elevation data when there is none. The ones we compiled by hand use their Wikipedia or Wikidata entry.
  • Winter closuresFrom the road’s seasonal closure tag in OpenStreetMap. If your route crosses a pass that may be closed this month, the app tells you.
  • BadgesPasses you ride over are ticked off from your ride log automatically. No internet needed.

Location and closure data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Photos from Wikimedia Commons and from riders; the app credits the author under each photo, and the licence too for Commons photos.

AI CoRider

A riding partner inside your helmet. Pro

AI CoRider is a riding partner that talks to you through your helmet. Press the button and ask: it knows your route, searches along it, adds stops and changes the route for you. You hear the answer in your helmet and your hands stay on the bars.

See AI CoRider in detail

What you can say

These are examples. There are no commands to memorise; say it in your own words.

  • How far to go? Is it going to rain on the way?

    Time left and rain

  • Any good place to eat on the way?

    Searches along your route, best-rated first

  • Add the first one as a stop.

    Adds the stop and updates the route

  • Let’s take the back roads.

    Switches the route mode

  • Will my fuel get me there?

    Works out your tank range

  • Where can I stay tonight?

    Looks for a place to stay

  • Tell the group we’re stopping for a break.

    Sends the group a ready-made alert

  • Remind me to oil the chain tomorrow.

    Saves the note and says it the next time you open it

How it works

  1. 1

    Press the button

    Tap the AI CoRider button on the map and start talking.

  2. 2

    Talk normally

    No commands to learn. It answers in the language you speak.

  3. 3

    Hear it in your helmet

    If your helmet is connected to the phone over Bluetooth, the voice comes through it. Can’t hear it in the wind? Just say “turn it up”.

  • Change the route by voice

    Adds, removes and skips stops. Changes your destination and puts stops in order.

  • Finds things ahead

    Looks for fuel, food or a place to stay up the road and mentions the best-rated first.

  • Weather and range

    Ask about the weather, rain on your route and how far your tank will take you. For range, enter once in Settings how far you ride on a full tank.

  • Keeps the group in touch

    Tells you where everyone is and sends the group a ready-made alert for you: falling behind, break, hazard or fuel.

  • Runs the ride

    Starts or ends navigation, turns on ride recording and takes you home.

  • Talk time

    Pro renews 10 minutes of talk time every 30 days, and the free trial includes 5. If you need more, add a 30, 60 or 180 minute pack that never expires.

  • Two modes

    “Planner” knows your route and gets things done; it comes with Pro. In “Chat” you pick the topic and it remembers earlier conversations; it opens with a minute pack.

  • In the group room

    Call AI CoRider into the group intercom and everyone in the room hears it. The minutes come from the account of whoever called it.

  • Remembers and calls

    Keeps the notes you ask for and forgets them when you say “forget that”. Finds someone in your contacts; one tap on the screen places the call.

Privacy. AI CoRider only runs when you turn it on. We don’t record or store your voice; what you say goes to a third-party cloud voice service so it can answer. Your raw location doesn’t go to that service; from your route it only gets text such as the distance left and the next manoeuvre. The session closes when you end it, after a while without speech, or when navigation ends. Details are in the privacy policy.

    Type the 8-character code or tap the link. Creating a group is part of Pro.

  • Location only while riding

    Your location is shared only while navigation or a ride recording is running, and sharing stops when you end it.

  • Open mic

  • Quick alerts to the group

    During a group ride the I’m behind, Break, Hazard and Fuel buttons send an alert to the group, visible for 10 minutes. Nothing is sent unless you tap.

More features

What else is in the app.

The things that didn’t fit anywhere else. All of them are in the App Store version.

  • Search along the route

    While navigating, search for Fuel, Coffee, Food, Groceries, Pharmacy, Rest area or Motorcycle repair, or type your own. For each result you see how far ahead it is, how many minutes it adds and which side of the road it’s on.

  • Rain warning

    The route screen checks four points along the route for your arrival time and warns you if rain is due. In My Tours every stop gets its own forecast.

  • Rain radar

    Turn on Settings → Map → Rain radar and the radar shows on the map in the USA and Germany. Elsewhere the button doesn’t appear.

  • Winter closure warning

    If your route crosses a pass that may be closed this month, it tells you before you set off. Generate a tour skips closed passes.

  • Fuel range

    Type how far a full tank takes you once, under Settings → Riding → Tank range. It warns you once when your range runs low, and notices when you fill up at a station.

  • Fuel estimate

    The route card shows the estimated fuel (litres, gallons in the US). Tap the consumption to enter your own.

  • Sunset note

    If the ride runs past sunset, the route card adds a line such as “Arrive 19:40 · sunset 19:12”.

  • Offline route copy

    Your tours keep a copy of their route on the phone. If the connection drops, the tour opens from that copy, and if you leave the route a “Back to route” arrow points the way. The map itself still needs internet.

  • When you leave the route

    Under Settings → Riding → When you leave the route you choose New route or Back to route, and you can let it skip a stop you’ve missed on its own.

  • Home and Work shortcuts

    Save your home and work addresses and go with one tap from the Start screen, where “A 60-minute loop from here” and a legendary road near you also turn up.

  • Battery saver screen

    While the next turn is far off, a black screen replaces the map and shows the turn, speed, limit and arrival. Under Settings → Riding you pick Off, With saver or Always.

  • A route from Google Maps

    Paste a Google Maps directions link into the search box, or use Share → Motoseyir in Google Maps, and the stops become a route.

  • Voice search

    Tap the microphone in the search box and say the address or place name.

  • Tour journal

    Take photos and leave a note at every stop, with up to three photos per stop. The journal stays on your phone, and up to three photos go with a tour you share in Community.

  • Trails and GPX

    Import a GPX file and the track lands in the Trails tab, with its distance, total climb, steepest gradient and elevation profile. Run your finger along the profile and the dot on the map follows.

  • Share a tour

    Send a tour as a link, a video or a GPX file. The link works for 30 days; the other person taps it, or goes to My Tours → With a code → Add tour. It’s free.

  • Community and Blog

    Community has Blog, Feed and My Ride Group tabs. In the feed you see other riders’ tours and rides, like, comment and reply, and filter by country. In Blog you can send your own post with Write a post. A username is all you need.

  • Events

    Create a meet-up in Community or tap “I’m going”. An event has a meeting point, a capacity and, if you like, a route; your location isn’t shared.

  • Add a photo to a pass

    On a pass page, Add photo lets you add your own (up to three per pass), credited under it with your username.

  • Landscape

    Turn the phone sideways and the cards gather on the left while the map takes the rest; My Tours switches to two columns.

  • Units and language

    Units follow where you are: miles, mph and feet in the US, miles and yards in the UK, kilometres elsewhere. The app speaks Turkish, English, German, French and Italian and follows your phone’s language.

Privacy

No account, no unnecessary data

You do not have to give a name, an email address or a phone number to use Motoseyir. What follows is a short summary of the privacy policy; the full policy is inside the app in English, under Settings → Privacy Policy.

  • You do not need to create an account — we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • To calculate your route, your location and search queries are passed to map services only; none of them store anything.
  • Your tours, ride recordings, journal notes and photos stay only on your device — unless you share them in Community.
  • An irreversible, anonymous identity hash of your device is sent to our server to stop trial and campaign entitlements being abused; that hash does not identify you.
  • If you turn on group riding, your last location is shared only while the ride is running and is deleted when it ends. Intercom audio is never recorded, only passed to whoever is in the room at that moment.
  • No ads; no data is shared with any advertising network.
