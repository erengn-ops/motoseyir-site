Motoseyir · The rider’s sat nav

The only sat nav that counts the kilometres.

Motoseyir works out the route by distance, not by minutes. It compares several alternatives between the same two points and suggests the shortest one. On a multi-stop tour it works out the order of the stops, too.

Start with a 7-day free trial on the 3-month or yearly plan.

Why it’s different

Other sat navs find the fastest road.
Motoseyir finds the shortest one.

A route calculated for a car always makes the same mistake on a bike: it shaves off minutes and adds kilometres. You filter through traffic, so that difference is real for you — more fuel, more tyre wear. Motoseyir compares the alternatives and suggests the shortest one, even if it takes a few minutes longer.

Features

One app for everything a rider does

Route, tour, ride and riding together — four headings, one app. The screens below are from the app itself.

Route

Shortest route

It calculates several alternative routes between the same two points and suggests the one with the shortest distance — even if it takes a little longer.

Tour modes

Back roads and Coast mode

In Back roads mode the route comes from our own server, which also uses the lanes a standard map skips. Ayder to Hemşin comes out at 24.1 km that way; general maps show 49.9 km for the same two points. Coast mode uses the same server to keep the route along the shoreline.

Multiple stops · Pro

Best order for your stops

You enter the delivery points in any order and “Best order” rearranges them for the shortest total distance. The example below is from a real ride: the same four stops, 57.1 km in your own order, 46.9 km in the order the app found.

Legendary routes

Legendary routes, ready to ride

Alpine passes like the Stelvio and the Sellaronda are ready and waiting, along with Turkish classics such as Şile–Ağva and the Lycian D400 and seven American ones, Tail of the Dragon and Big Sur included. Open one from the list and it goes straight into your tours with its stops and its route.

Riding together

Group ride, Invite to ride and intercom

When one person creates a group a single code is generated, everyone joins with that code, and you all see each other on the route. In My Ride Group, “Invite to ride” next to a friend sends your route and a voice link in one tap; they answer with “Ride the same route” or “Riding behind you”.

The intercom also starts without a route. Each friend’s volume is set separately and Bluetooth helmets connect directly. Nothing is recorded — the audio goes only to whoever is in the room at that moment, and the room closes when the ride ends.

My Tours

Tour journal

At every stop you take a photo and leave a short note, and both stay under that stop. The photos also show up as cards on the tour map; tap one to open it full screen. The journal stays on your phone; if you choose to share it in Community, at most three photos go with it.

Weather

Rain warning along the route

If rain is expected on your route, it warns you in advance for the time you will actually get there. If the weather is dry, nothing appears on screen.

My Rides

Ride recording and trails

Ride recording keeps running with the screen off, and the track is coloured by speed. You can send a finished ride to your group: the track, distance and time stay there for 14 days. For a trail you brought in as GPX it works out the distance, total climb and steepest gradient; as you slide your finger along the profile, the point on the map moves with it.

Discover

Discover

It lists the coves, campsites and places to eat around your route. You look at the rating and how far it is from your route, then add the one you like as a stop.

Also in the app

  • Fuel cost

    You see the estimated fuel cost of the route before you set off. It calculates in litres per 100 km where that is the local unit, and in miles per gallon in the US.

  • Voice guidance

    Turn instructions are spoken by your phone’s voice in the app’s language; with the app in Turkish they come from a recorded human voice-over instead. Distances follow your region — “In 500 feet, turn right” in the US.

  • Community feed

    The tours you share appear in the feed with your username and icon, and others can like and comment on them. Posts are reviewed before they go live. Around 400 metres near your start and finish are trimmed before anything is shared.

  • Landscape mode

    Turn the phone sideways and the map, cards and buttons switch to a new layout: in navigation the turn and arrival cards sit on the left and the map on the right; My Tours shows two columns.

  • Rider markers

    You pick your marker from the grid in Settings: bike, chopper, maxi scooter, touring, arrow or helmet. The three added in the latest update show the rider as well.

Coming soon: AI CoRider — a riding companion you can talk to inside your helmet. Not in the App Store version yet; it arrives in Pro in a later update.

Your data

No account, no unnecessary data

You do not have to give a name, an email address or a phone number to use Motoseyir. What follows is a short summary of the privacy policy; the full policy is inside the app in English, under Settings → Privacy Policy.

  • You do not need to create an account — we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • To calculate your route, your location and search queries are passed to map services only; none of them store anything.
  • Your tours, ride recordings, journal notes and photos stay only on your device — unless you share them in Community.
  • An irreversible, anonymous identity hash of your device is sent to our server to stop trial and campaign entitlements being abused; that hash does not identify you.
  • If you turn on group riding, your last location is shared only while the ride is running and is deleted when it ends. Intercom audio is never recorded, only passed to whoever is in the room at that moment.
  • No ads; no data is shared with any advertising network.

What changed

Every major update is here, newest first.

1.1.0 September 2026
  • Landscape mode: turn the phone sideways and the map, cards and buttons switch to a new layout. In navigation the turn and arrival cards sit on the left, the map on the right; My Tours shows two columns.
