Passo del San Bernardino by motorcycle

Passo del San Bernardino tops out at 2,067 m (6,781 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 16th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Switzerland

Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to April, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
