Col Sant’Angelo by motorcycle

Col Sant’Angelo tops out at 1,756 m (5,761 ft) in Italy. It is the 53rd highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
