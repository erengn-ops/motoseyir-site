Spitzingsattel by motorcycle

Spitzingsattel tops out at 1,130 m (3,707 ft) in Germany. It is the 5th highest of the 17 passes in Germany on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
