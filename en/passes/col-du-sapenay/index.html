Col du Sapenay by motorcycle

Col du Sapenay tops out at 897 m (2,943 ft) in France. It is the 122nd highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to April, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
