Mont du Chat by motorcycle

Mont du Chat tops out at 1,486 m (4,875 ft) in France. It is the 45th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
1,486 m (4,875 ft)
Location
France
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.

45.6610, 5.8216
Sources

Ride a loop over Mont du Chat

The web route planner opens with Mont du Chat as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
