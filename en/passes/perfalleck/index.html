Perfalleck by motorcycle

Perfalleck tops out at 822 m (2,697 ft) in Austria. It is the 112th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Perfalleck

The web route planner opens with Perfalleck as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
