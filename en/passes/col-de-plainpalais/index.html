Col de Plainpalais by motorcycle

Col de Plainpalais tops out at 1,173 m (3,848 ft) in France. It is the 73rd highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
