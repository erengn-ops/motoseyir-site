Col de Saxel by motorcycle

Col de Saxel tops out at 943 m (3,094 ft) in France. It is the 115th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
