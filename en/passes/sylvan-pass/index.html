Sylvan Pass by motorcycle

Sylvan Pass tops out at 8,530 ft (2,600 m) in Wyoming. It is the 7th highest of the 12 passes in Wyoming on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
8,530 ft (2,600 m)
Location
Wyoming, United States
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to April, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
44.4645, -110.1272
Sources
Wikipedia · OpenStreetMap

Elevation is the figure on the pass’s OpenStreetMap entry or its source page; the point is the summit on the road itself.

Ride a loop over Sylvan Pass

The web route planner opens with Sylvan Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Sylvan Pass in the Motoseyir app
