Arlbergpass by motorcycle

Arlbergpass tops out at 1,793 m (5,883 ft) in Austria. It is the 9th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Arlbergpass

The web route planner opens with Arlbergpass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Arlbergpass in the Motoseyir app
