Schiestelscharte by motorcycle

Schiestelscharte tops out at 2,024 m (6,640 ft) in Austria. It is the 5th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
