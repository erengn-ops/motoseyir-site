Col du Coq by motorcycle

Col du Coq tops out at 1,434 m (4,705 ft) in France. It is the 49th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

1,434 m (4,705 ft)
Location
France
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to April, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
