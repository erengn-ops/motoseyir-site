Cook and Green Pass by motorcycle

Cook and Green Pass tops out at 4,738 ft (1,444 m) in California. It is the 36th highest of the 56 passes in California on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Cook and Green Pass

The web route planner opens with Cook and Green Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
