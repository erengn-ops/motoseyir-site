Colter Pass by motorcycle

Colter Pass tops out at 8,048 ft (2,453 m) in Montana. It is the highest of the 10 passes in Montana on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
8,048 ft (2,453 m)
Location
Montana, United States
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
45.0267, -109.8909
Sources
OpenStreetMap

Elevation is the figure on the pass’s OpenStreetMap entry or its source page; the point is the summit on the road itself.

Ride a loop over Colter Pass

The web route planner opens with Colter Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Colter Pass in the Motoseyir app
