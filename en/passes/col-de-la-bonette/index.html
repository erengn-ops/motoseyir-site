Col de la Bonette by motorcycle

Col de la Bonette tops out at 2,715 m (8,907 ft) in France. It is the 3rd highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

2,715 m (8,907 ft)
Location
France
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
