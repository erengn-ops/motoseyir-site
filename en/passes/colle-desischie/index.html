Colle d'Esischie by motorcycle

Colle d'Esischie tops out at 2,370 m (7,776 ft) in Italy. It is the 10th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
