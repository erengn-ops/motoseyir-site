Bozeman Pass by motorcycle

Bozeman Pass tops out at 5,719 ft (1,743 m) in Montana. It is the 8th highest of the 10 passes in Montana on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Bozeman Pass

The web route planner opens with Bozeman Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
