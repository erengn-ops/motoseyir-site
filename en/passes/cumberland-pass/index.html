Cumberland Pass by motorcycle

Cumberland Pass tops out at 12,014 ft (3,662 m) in Colorado. It is the 3rd highest of the 63 passes in Colorado on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
