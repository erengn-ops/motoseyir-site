Passo Cereda by motorcycle

Passo Cereda tops out at 1,369 m (4,491 ft) in Italy. It is the 88th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
