Les Agites by motorcycle

Les Agites tops out at 1,569 m (5,148 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 30th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
