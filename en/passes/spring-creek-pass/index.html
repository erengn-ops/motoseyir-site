Spring Creek Pass by motorcycle

Spring Creek Pass tops out at 10,889 ft (3,319 m) in Colorado. It is the 21st highest of the 63 passes in Colorado on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
