Grafton Notch by motorcycle

Grafton Notch tops out at 1,542 ft (470 m) in Maine. It is the highest of the 2 passes in Maine on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
