Soco Gap by motorcycle

Soco Gap tops out at 4,341 ft (1,323 m) in North Carolina. It is the 4th highest of the 14 passes in North Carolina on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Soco Gap

The web route planner opens with Soco Gap as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
