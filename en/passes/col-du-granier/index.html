Col du Granier by motorcycle

Col du Granier tops out at 1,134 m (3,720 ft) in France. It is the 83rd highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
