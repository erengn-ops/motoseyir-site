Gotthardpass by motorcycle

Gotthardpass tops out at 2,106 m (6,909 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 15th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Gotthardpass

The web route planner opens with Gotthardpass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
