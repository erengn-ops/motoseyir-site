Scott Mountain Summit by motorcycle

Scott Mountain Summit tops out at 5,407 ft (1,648 m) in California. It is the 27th highest of the 56 passes in California on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
