Crooked Summit by motorcycle

Crooked Summit tops out at 4,524 ft (1,379 m) in Idaho. It is the 24th highest of the 28 passes in Idaho on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
