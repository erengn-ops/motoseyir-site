Dientner Sattel by motorcycle

Dientner Sattel tops out at 1,370 m (4,495 ft) in Austria. It is the 35th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
