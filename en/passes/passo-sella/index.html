Passo Sella by motorcycle

Passo Sella tops out at 2,218 m (7,277 ft) in Italy. It is the 17th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
