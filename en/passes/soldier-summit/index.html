Soldier Summit by motorcycle

Soldier Summit tops out at 7,477 ft (2,279 m) in Utah. It is the 5th highest of the 6 passes in Utah on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
