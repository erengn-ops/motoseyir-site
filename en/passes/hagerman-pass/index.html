Hagerman Pass by motorcycle

Hagerman Pass tops out at 11,926 ft (3,635 m) in Colorado. It is the 5th highest of the 63 passes in Colorado on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
