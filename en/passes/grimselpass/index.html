Grimselpass by motorcycle

Grimselpass tops out at 2,164 m (7,100 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 12th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Location
Switzerland
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.

Ride a loop over Grimselpass

The web route planner opens with Grimselpass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Grimselpass in the Motoseyir app
