Passo Valparola by motorcycle

Passo Valparola tops out at 2,168 m (7,113 ft) in Italy. It is the 23rd highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
