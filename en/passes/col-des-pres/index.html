Col des Prés by motorcycle

Col des Prés tops out at 1,134 m (3,720 ft) in France. It is the 82nd highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
