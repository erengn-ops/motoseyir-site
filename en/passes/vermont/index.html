Mountain passes in Vermont

The Motoseyir pass map has 7 passes in Vermont. Lincoln Gap is the highest at 2,448 ft. 1 of them are on unpaved roads. For 3 we know the months the road is usually closed to motor vehicles. Open a pass for its location, the passes around it and a loop that rides over it.
