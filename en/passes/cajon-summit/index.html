Cajon Summit by motorcycle

Cajon Summit tops out at 4,259 ft (1,298 m) in California. It is the 39th highest of the 56 passes in California on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
