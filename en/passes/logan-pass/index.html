Logan Pass by motorcycle

Logan Pass tops out at 6,644 ft (2,025 m) in Montana. It is the 6th highest of the 10 passes in Montana on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
6,644 ft (2,025 m)
Location
Montana, United States
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to June, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
48.6966, -113.7180
Sources
Wikipedia · OpenStreetMap

Elevation is the figure on the pass’s OpenStreetMap entry or its source page; the point is the summit on the road itself.

Ride a loop over Logan Pass

The web route planner opens with Logan Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Logan Pass in the Motoseyir app

Logan Pass is on Motoseyir’s pass map with its altitude and exact location, along with 1,600+ passes in 36 countries. Add it to your tour and go.

If your route crosses a pass that may be closed this month, the app tells you before you set off, and Generate a tour skips closed passes. Passes you ride over are ticked off from your ride log automatically.
