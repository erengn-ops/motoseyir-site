Lemhi Pass by motorcycle

Lemhi Pass tops out at 7,372 ft (2,247 m) in Idaho. It is the 2nd highest of the 28 passes in Idaho on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
