Schönfeldsattel by motorcycle

Schönfeldsattel tops out at 1,741 m (5,712 ft) in Austria. It is the 13th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
