Ziehberg by motorcycle

Ziehberg tops out at 683 m (2,241 ft) in Austria. It is the 131st highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
