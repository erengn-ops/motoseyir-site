Buchenegg by motorcycle

Buchenegg tops out at 786 m (2,579 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 65th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
