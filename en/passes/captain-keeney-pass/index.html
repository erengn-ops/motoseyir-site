Captain Keeney Pass by motorcycle

Captain Keeney Pass tops out at 2,887 ft (880 m) in Oregon. It is the 22nd highest of the 22 passes in Oregon on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
