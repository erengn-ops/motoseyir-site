Äulemer Kreuz by motorcycle

Äulemer Kreuz tops out at 1,138 m (3,734 ft) in Germany. It is the 4th highest of the 17 passes in Germany on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
