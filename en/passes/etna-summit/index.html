Etna Summit by motorcycle

Etna Summit tops out at 5,955 ft (1,815 m) in California. It is the 23rd highest of the 56 passes in California on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

The web route planner opens with Etna Summit as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
