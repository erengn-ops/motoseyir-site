Bobcat Pass by motorcycle

Bobcat Pass tops out at 9,810 ft (2,990 m) in New Mexico. It is the highest of the 9 passes in New Mexico on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
