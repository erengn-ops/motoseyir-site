Colle delle Finestre by motorcycle

Colle delle Finestre tops out at 2,176 m (7,139 ft) in Italy. It is the 22nd highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Location
Italy
Road surface
Unpaved (dirt or gravel)
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
