Präbichl by motorcycle

Präbichl tops out at 1,226 m (4,022 ft) in Austria. It is the 51st highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
