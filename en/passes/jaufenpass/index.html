Jaufenpass by motorcycle

Jaufenpass tops out at 2,094 m (6,870 ft) in Italy. It is the 27th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
