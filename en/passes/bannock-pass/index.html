Bannock Pass by motorcycle

Bannock Pass tops out at 7,684 ft (2,342 m) in Montana. It is the 2nd highest of the 10 passes in Montana on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Bannock Pass

The web route planner opens with Bannock Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
