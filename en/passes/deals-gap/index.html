Deals Gap by motorcycle

Deals Gap tops out at 1,988 ft (606 m) in Tennessee. It is the 3rd highest of the 3 passes in Tennessee on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
