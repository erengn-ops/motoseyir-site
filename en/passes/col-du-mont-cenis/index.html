Col du Mont-Cenis by motorcycle

Col du Mont-Cenis tops out at 2,081 m (6,827 ft) in France. It is the 14th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
