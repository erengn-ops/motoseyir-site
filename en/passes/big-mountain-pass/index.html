Big Mountain Pass by motorcycle

Big Mountain Pass tops out at 7,421 ft (2,262 m) in Utah. It is the 6th highest of the 6 passes in Utah on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

The web route planner opens with Big Mountain Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
