Črnivec by motorcycle

Črnivec tops out at 902 m (2,959 ft) in Slovenia. It is the 6th highest of the 9 passes in Slovenia on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
