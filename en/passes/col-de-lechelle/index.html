Col de l'Échelle by motorcycle

Col de l'Échelle tops out at 1,762 m (5,781 ft) in France. It is the 27th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

France
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
