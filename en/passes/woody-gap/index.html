Woody Gap by motorcycle

Woody Gap tops out at 3,176 ft (968 m) in Georgia. It is the highest of the 3 passes in Georgia on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Woody Gap

The web route planner opens with Woody Gap as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
