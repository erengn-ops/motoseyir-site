Goshen Pass by motorcycle

Goshen Pass tops out at 1,148 ft (350 m) in Virginia. It is the 9th highest of the 12 passes in Virginia on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
