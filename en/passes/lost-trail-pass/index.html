Lost Trail Pass by motorcycle

Lost Trail Pass tops out at 7,014 ft (2,138 m) in Montana. It is the 5th highest of the 9 passes in Montana on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Lost Trail Pass

The web route planner opens with Lost Trail Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
