Guardsman Pass by motorcycle

Guardsman Pass tops out at 9,718 ft (2,962 m) in Utah. It is the 3rd highest of the 6 passes in Utah on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
9,718 ft (2,962 m)
Location
Utah, United States
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
40.6067, -111.5549
Sources
Wikipedia · OpenStreetMap
