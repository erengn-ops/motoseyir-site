Oberalppass by motorcycle

Oberalppass tops out at 2,044 m (6,706 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 17th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Location
Switzerland
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from December to March, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.

Ride a loop over Oberalppass

The web route planner opens with Oberalppass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Oberalppass in the Motoseyir app

Oberalppass is on Motoseyir’s pass map with its altitude and exact location, along with 1,600+ passes in 36 countries. Add it to your tour and go.

If your route crosses a pass that may be closed this month, the app tells you before you set off, and Generate a tour skips closed passes. Passes you ride over are ticked off from your ride log automatically.
