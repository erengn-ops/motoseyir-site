Great St Bernard Pass by motorcycle

Great St Bernard Pass tops out at 2,469 m (8,100 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 3rd highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
2,469 m (8,100 ft)
Location
Switzerland, Italy
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from October to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
45.8691, 7.1704
Sources
Wikipedia · OpenStreetMap

Elevation is the figure on the pass’s OpenStreetMap entry or its source page; the point is the summit on the road itself.

Ride a loop over Great St Bernard Pass

The web route planner opens with Great St Bernard Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.
