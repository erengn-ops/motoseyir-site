Newfound Gap by motorcycle

Newfound Gap tops out at 5,049 ft (1,539 m) in North Carolina. It is the 3rd highest of the 14 passes in North Carolina on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
