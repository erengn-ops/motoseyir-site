Penser Joch by motorcycle

Penser Joch tops out at 2,211 m (7,254 ft) in Italy. It is the 18th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Location
Italy
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
