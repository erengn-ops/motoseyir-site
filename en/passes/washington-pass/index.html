Washington Pass by motorcycle

Washington Pass tops out at 5,476 ft (1,669 m) in Washington. It is the 2nd highest of the 17 passes in Washington on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

5,476 ft (1,669 m)
Location
Washington, United States
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to May, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.

48.5236, -120.6542
