Old Rabbit Ears Pass by motorcycle

Old Rabbit Ears Pass tops out at 9,573 ft (2,918 m) in Colorado. It is the 47th highest of the 63 passes in Colorado on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
