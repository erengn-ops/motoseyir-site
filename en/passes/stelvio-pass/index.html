Stelvio Pass by motorcycle

Stelvio Pass tops out at 2,758 m (9,049 ft) in Italy. It is the highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

At a glance

Elevation
2,758 m (9,049 ft)
Location
Italy
Road surface
Paved
Winter
Usually closed to motor vehicles from November to April, going by the seasonal closure recorded for the road. Dates move with the snow every year, so check the road authority’s current notice before you go.
Coordinates
46.5286, 10.4532
Sources
Wikipedia · OpenStreetMap

Elevation is the figure on the pass’s OpenStreetMap entry or its source page; the point is the summit on the road itself.

Ride a loop over Stelvio Pass

The web route planner opens with Stelvio Pass as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Stelvio Pass in the Motoseyir app
