Criterion Summit by motorcycle

Criterion Summit tops out at 3,363 ft (1,025 m) in Oregon. It is the 20th highest of the 21 passes in Oregon on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
