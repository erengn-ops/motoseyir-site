Postojnska vrata by motorcycle

Postojnska vrata tops out at 606 m (1,988 ft) in Slovenia. It is the 9th highest of the 9 passes in Slovenia on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
