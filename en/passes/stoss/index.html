Stoss by motorcycle

Stoss tops out at 943 m (3,094 ft) in Switzerland. It is the 57th highest of the 74 passes in Switzerland on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.

Ride a loop over Stoss

The web route planner opens with Stoss as the start point. Choose a direction, how long you want to ride and how twisty the roads should be, and it draws up to three round trips that come back to the pass. Send the one you like to your phone with a QR code and ride it in Motoseyir.

Stoss in the Motoseyir app
