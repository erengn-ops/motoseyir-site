Colle del Nivolet by motorcycle

Colle del Nivolet tops out at 2,612 m (8,570 ft) in Italy. It is the 4th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
