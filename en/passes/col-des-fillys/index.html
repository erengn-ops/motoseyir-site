Col des Fillys by motorcycle

Col des Fillys tops out at 1,322 m (4,337 ft) in France. It is the 56th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
