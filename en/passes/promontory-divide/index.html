Promontory Divide by motorcycle

Promontory Divide tops out at 8,579 ft (2,615 m) in Colorado. It is the 60th highest of the 63 passes in Colorado on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
