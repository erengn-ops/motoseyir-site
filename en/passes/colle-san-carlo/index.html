Colle San Carlo by motorcycle

Colle San Carlo tops out at 1,951 m (6,401 ft) in Italy. It is the 39th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
