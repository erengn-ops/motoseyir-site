Wards Fork Gap by motorcycle

Wards Fork Gap tops out at 5,358 ft (1,633 m) in California. It is the 29th highest of the 56 passes in California on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
