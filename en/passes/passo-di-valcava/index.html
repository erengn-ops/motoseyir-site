Passo di Valcava by motorcycle

Passo di Valcava tops out at 1,340 m (4,396 ft) in Italy. It is the 92nd highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
