Col du Pilon by motorcycle

Col du Pilon tops out at 782 m (2,566 ft) in France. It is the 128th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
