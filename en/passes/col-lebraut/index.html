Col Lebraut by motorcycle

Col Lebraut tops out at 1,110 m (3,642 ft) in France. It is the 90th highest of the 145 passes in France on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
