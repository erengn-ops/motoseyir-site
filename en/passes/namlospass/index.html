Namlospass by motorcycle

Namlospass tops out at 1,359 m (4,459 ft) in Austria. It is the 37th highest of the 160 passes in Austria on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
