Mountain passes in numbers

The Motoseyir pass database lists 1,635 mountain passes in 36 countries. They range from Çetibeli Geçidi at 170 m to Hushang (Ak-Baital) Pass at 4,655 m, and the median pass lies at 1,216 m. This page shows the numbers behind the pass pages on this site and ends with a line you can copy when you cite them.

Source and method

The pass list is the one inside the Motoseyir app. It combines a hand-kept list with compilations from OpenStreetMap, which map named passes on roads that can be ridden; most entries point to a Wikipedia or Wikidata page. Elevation is the value in that data, in metres.

Surface (unpaved) and usual closing months appear only where the data has them. US entries were checked against Wikipedia coordinates, and on 28 October 2026 three entries whose tags had been copied from another pass were removed.

The counts show how many passes are in this database, not how many passes a country has. They depend on what is mapped and tagged, so a country with a low count may simply be less covered.

Frequently asked questions

  • How many mountain passes does Motoseyir list?

    1,635 passes in 36 countries, in the data snapshot of 27 October 2026. 295 of them are in the United States, 535 in the Alps and 115 in Türkiye.

  • Which is the highest pass in the Motoseyir database?

    Hushang (Ak-Baital) Pass in Tajikistan at 4,655 m. The lowest entry is Çetibeli Geçidi in Türkiye at 170 m, and the median is 1,216 m.

  • Does the list show every pass in a country?

    No. It reflects what the app’s pass data and its OpenStreetMap sources contain, so a country’s count tells you about data coverage, not about how many passes exist there.
