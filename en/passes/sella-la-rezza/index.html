Sella la Rezza by motorcycle

Sella la Rezza tops out at 430 m (1,411 ft) in Italy. It is the 147th highest of the 149 passes in Italy on the Motoseyir map. Below: the road surface, when it is usually closed, the passes around it and a loop you can ride over it.
