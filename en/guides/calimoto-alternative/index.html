How we score

Twelve criteria, each scored 0, 1, 3 or 5 by a fixed rule, then weighted. The weights add up to 100, so the total is out of 100: weight × score ÷ 5, summed. Curvy road choice carries the most weight because it is the reason most riders install a motorcycle app at all.

CriterionWeightHow 0–5 is given
Curvy or scenic road choice155 = two or more curviness levels for an A-to-B route · 3 = one curvy, scenic or back-road mode · 0 = not stated
Avoid motorways55 = explicit option to avoid motorways · 3 = a routing mode that skips them · 0 = not stated
Round trip generator105 = you choose length and direction · 3 = length (or time) only · 0 = not stated

Prices, App Store ratings, the Turkish interface and what the free version does are shown but not scored. Prices differ by country and by offer, and a user rating measures something else.

Motoseyir leads with 88, calimoto follows with 83. Weigh the criteria differently and the order changes. If curvy routing and Android Auto are what you care about, calimoto and Kurviger come out ahead of Motoseyir.

Where Motoseyir is ahead, and where it is behind

  • Ahead: voice turn-by-turn navigation in the free version, without a limit; a group ride with an intercom and the AI CoRider voice assistant; a database of 1,638 mountain passes in 36 countries; round trips by direction, length and twistiness.
  • Behind: no Android Auto; outside Türkiye, Europe and the USA, routing and navigation need Pro; one small-road mode instead of several curviness levels; speed camera alerts only in Türkiye.
  • App Store ratings: far fewer than the others so far (see below).

Which app suits which rider

  • You want the curviest road

    calimoto and Kurviger offer more than one curviness level for an A-to-B route. Motoseyir's default is the shortest distance; Back roads is its one small-road mode.

  • Android Auto is a must

    Pick calimoto, Kurviger or Scenic; all three list Android Auto. Motoseyir has Apple CarPlay on iPhone and no Android Auto.

  • You ride far from home

    All five apps route anywhere in the world. Motoseyir's routing and navigation are free in Türkiye, Europe and the USA; elsewhere they are part of Pro.

  • You don't want to pay for navigation

    Motoseyir gives voice turn-by-turn navigation free and without a limit in its three regions. The other four keep turn-by-turn in the paid plan.

  • You ride with friends

    Motoseyir and calimoto put the whole group live on one map. Motoseyir adds an intercom over the internet and the AI CoRider voice assistant.

  • You ride off-road or more than a motorcycle

    REVER covers ATVs, snowmobiles and 4x4s with its off-road planner, and Scenic has an unpaved routing mode.

What each developer says its app does

calimoto

Made by calimoto GmbH. Four routing profiles (Fast, No highway, Winding, Twisty), a Round Trip Planner with eight directions, GPX, KML and ITN import, and a ride tracker with lean angle. Its pricing page puts turn-by-turn navigation, offline maps, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, synchronised group rides with location sharing and speed camera alerts in Premium.

Kurviger

KURVIGER GmbH publishes Kurviger. Its planner lets you set how curvy the route is and exclude motorways, main roads, toll roads, ferries and unpaved roads. Round trips go up to 300 km free and 600 km with Tourer; navigation, offline maps and ride recording are in Tourer+. The website lists Android Auto and Apple CarPlay.

Scenic

Scenic comes from Applified Life Ltd. Its Premium page lists turn-by-turn voice navigation, offline maps, round trip generation, route import and GPX export, and speed and red-light camera alerts; the free version follows the route line, and Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are listed on the same page.

REVER

REVER is published by Rever, LLC and covers motorcycles, ATVs, snowmobiles and 4x4s. PRO adds Twisty Routing, turn-by-turn directions, offline maps, GPX export and live location sharing; GPX import and route line navigation are free.

Motoseyir

Motoseyir picks the route by distance rather than minutes and puts several stops in the best order. Routing and voice navigation are free and unlimited in Türkiye, Europe and the USA, without an account. Pro adds Back roads and Coast modes, round trips, offline maps, Apple CarPlay, creating a group ride with intercom and AI CoRider.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is a good calimoto alternative?

    Kurviger is closest if you want curvy routing worldwide with Android Auto. Motoseyir scores highest on our criteria because navigation is free, it has group rides with an intercom and a voice assistant, and a pass database, but it has no Android Auto, and outside Türkiye, Europe and the USA navigation needs Pro.

  • How were the apps scored?

    Twelve criteria, each scored 0, 1, 3 or 5 by a published rule, weighted to a total out of 100. Only what the developer states on its own App Store page or website counts; a feature that is not mentioned scores 0.

  • Is Motoseyir free?

    Routing and voice turn-by-turn navigation are free and unlimited in Türkiye, Europe and the USA, without an account. Round trips, offline maps, CarPlay, creating a group ride and AI CoRider are part of Pro, from $8.99 a month or $39.99 a year on the US App Store.

  • Does Motoseyir support Android Auto?

    No. Motoseyir runs on Android phones, but its car-screen support is Apple CarPlay on iPhone.
