Draw your own trip

In Motoseyir a trip starts with a start and an end point, and you place the stops in between yourself. Add a stop from the search box or by long-pressing an empty spot on the map, so even a place without a name can be a stop. Reorder the stops however you like.

The app plans by distance rather than time. Route calculation and navigation are free and unlimited in Turkey, Europe and the US, and you do not need an account.

If you want a starting point, ready-made routes are in the Legendary tab: tap one and its stops are copied into your trips, ready to edit.
