Motoseyir Pro · AI CoRider

Eyes on the road. It’s listening.

AI CoRider is the riding partner who talks to you through your helmet headset. It knows your route: it adds stops, finds places along the way and changes the route for you. Press the button, ask in your own words, and hear the answer in your helmet. Your hands stay on the bars.

How it works

Press, talk, hear it in your helmet.

  1. 1

    Press the button

    Tap the AI CoRider button on the map and start talking.

  2. 2

    Talk normally

    No commands to learn. It answers in the language you speak.

  3. 3

    Hear it in your helmet

    If your helmet is connected to the phone over Bluetooth, the voice comes through it. Can’t hear it in the wind? Just say “turn it up”.

What it does

About 30 actions by voice.

Grouped into five. You don’t have to memorise any of them; just say what you want in your own words.

01

Route

It fixes the route for you.

  • Adds a stop, removes one or skips the next
  • Changes the destination and puts the stops in order
  • Switches between back roads and the shortest route
  • Shows the route on the map, takes you home
02

On the road

It searches along your route and around you.

  • Finds fuel, food or coffee ahead and names the best rated first
  • Looks for somewhere to stay tonight
  • Tells you the time left, the next turn and where you are
  • Tells you if there are sharp bends or climbs ahead
03

Weather and range

What you ask before and during the ride.

  • What the weather will be like, whether it rains on your route
  • Checks the rain radar and opens it on the map
  • How far your tank will go, whether there’s fuel on the way
  • The rain radar works only in the US and Germany. For range, enter once in Settings the distance you rode on a full tank.
04

Group

When a group ride is on.

  • Tells you where everyone is and who has fallen behind
  • Sends the group a ready-made alert: I’m behind, break, danger, fuel
  • If you shared the route with the group, it asks who a change applies to
05

Ride

While your hands stay on the bars.

  • Starts and ends navigation
  • Starts and stops ride recording
  • Finds someone in your contacts; one tap on the screen places the call
  • Keeps a note you ask for, deletes it if you say “forget”
  • Raises or lowers its own volume: “turn it up”

In the group room

Call AI CoRider into the group intercom and everyone in the room hears it. The minutes come from the account of whoever called it.

Privacy

Your voice stays yours.

  • It works when you start it

    The microphone opens only when you tap the AI CoRider button on the map.

  • Voice isn’t stored

    Your voice isn’t recorded by us. It goes to a third-party cloud voice service to produce the answer.

  • Raw location stays out

    Only text, such as the distance left and the next manoeuvre, leaves the route.

  • Notes on your phone

    The notes you ask it to keep are stored on your phone; say “forget” and they’re deleted.

Privacy. AI CoRider only runs when you turn it on. We don’t record or store your voice; what you say goes to a third-party cloud voice service so it can answer. Your raw location doesn’t go to that service; from your route it only gets text such as the distance left and the next manoeuvre. The session closes when you end it, after a while without speech, or when navigation ends. Details are in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Is AI CoRider always listening?

No. There’s no wake word. The microphone opens only when you tap the AI CoRider button on the map. The session closes when you end it, after a stretch of silence, or when navigation ends.

Does it work without internet?

No. What you say goes to a cloud voice service to produce the answer, so AI CoRider needs an internet connection. Without one, a session can’t start.

Which languages can I speak?

It answers in the language you speak. There are no commands to learn; say it in your own words.

What happens when my talk time runs out?

Pro renews 10 minutes every 30 days, and the free trial includes 5. If that isn’t enough, add a 30, 60 or 180 minute pack that never expires.

What’s the difference between Planner and Chat?

Planner knows your route and gets things done: it adds stops, finds places and changes the route, and it’s included in Pro. In Chat you pick the topic and it remembers earlier chats; it opens with a minute pack.

Does everyone in a group hear it, and whose minutes are used?

If you call AI CoRider into the group intercom, everyone in the room hears it. The minutes come from the account of whoever called it.

I ask if my fuel will last, but it gives no range. Why?

For range, enter once in Settings the distance you rode on a full tank. Until you do, AI CoRider doesn’t guess; it tells you so.

Do I need Bluetooth to hear it in my helmet?

If your helmet is connected to the phone over Bluetooth, the voice comes through it; otherwise it plays from the phone. Can’t hear it in the wind? Just say “turn it up”.
