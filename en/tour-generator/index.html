How the route generator works

  1. Step 1

    Set the start

    Search for a town or address, tap the map or use your location. You can drag the pin afterwards.

    Try it now

  2. Step 2

    Choose direction, length and curves

    Pick one of eight directions or leave it on any direction for a random loop. Set the length in kilometres, miles or hours, then choose Relaxed, Twisty or Very twisty. Skip motorways, tolls or dirt roads if you want to.

    Try it now

  3. Step 3

    Generate

    The generator checks many loops on real roads and keeps the best three. Tap Another one for different loops.

    Try it now

  4. Step 4

    Send it to your phone

    Scan the QR code or open the link. The tour lands in My Tours in the Motoseyir app, ready for turn-by-turn navigation.

    Try it now

What the Tour generator does in the app

You pick the start on the map. Turning and stretching the dial's handle sets direction and length, then you choose Relaxed, Twisty or Very twisty. The app tries many tours on real roads and shows the best three.

  • Pick one of eight directions or leave it open in every direction.
  • Set the length in kilometres, miles or hours.
  • Leave out motorways, tolls and unpaved roads, or favour tours over a mountain pass.
  • Browse viewpoints, food, camping and bays along the tour and add the ones you like as stops.

How it differs from the web route generator

In the route generator in your browser you draw the tour and send it to your phone with a QR code. In the app you edit the tour directly, ride it with navigation and save it. Both use the same planning logic.

Frequently asked questions

  • Is the Tour generator free?

    The Motoseyir app is free to download. Some features, such as the country-roads mode, are in Motoseyir Pro, and the free tier keeps one saved tour.

  • Which phones have the Tour generator?

    Motoseyir is on the App Store for iPhone for now. The Android version is not on Google Play yet.

  • Can I try it in the browser?

    Yes. The route generator in your browser uses the same dial, draws the tour and sends it to your phone with a QR code.
