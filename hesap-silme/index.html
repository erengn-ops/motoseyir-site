Delete your Motoseyir data

This page explains how to delete your data from Motoseyir, the motorcycle navigation app by Batuhan Eren Engin (Google Play and App Store). Motoseyir does not ask for your name, email address or password. The only account is the optional Community membership, which is tied to your phone and a username you choose.

In the app

  1. Community membership: open Community › My profile › “Delete membership”. Your username, icon or profile photo, shared tours, comments, likes, forum questions and answers, event participations, the photos you added to mountain passes and your leaderboard entry are deleted from our server immediately.
  2. Everything else: open Settings › “Delete my data”. Your tours, ride recordings, journal links, offline maps and preferences are deleted from the phone; your community membership and posts, support chat, notification record, group membership and last shared location are deleted from our server.

Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the phone.

By email

If you no longer have the app or the phone, write to erengn@gmail.com with the subject “Motoseyir data deletion”. Tell us your Community username if you had one. We delete the data within 30 days and reply when it is done. On request we also delete the remaining records tied to your device ID digest: trial and offer records, AI CoRider session and minute-pack records, and biker-friendly hotel and mechanic suggestions you sent.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play or the App Store, not by us; we cannot delete them. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Subscriptions or in your Apple ID settings.
  • Crash reports in Sentry contain no name, email or location and are deleted automatically when Sentry’s retention period ends.
  • Data that expires on its own: group ride locations when the ride ends, tour codes after 30 days, ride recordings sent to a group after 14 days, support chats after one year.

Türkçe: Verilerinizi silme

Topluluk üyeliğinizi Topluluk › Profilim › “Üyeliği sil” ile silersiniz: kullanıcı adınız, paylaşımlarınız, yorumlarınız, beğenileriniz, forum sorularınız ve cevaplarınız ve geçitlere eklediğiniz fotoğraflar sunucudan hemen silinir. Ayarlar › “Verilerimi sil” ise telefondaki turları, sürüş kayıtlarını ve tercihleri siler; sunucudaki üyeliğinizi, destek sohbetinizi, bildirim kaydınızı ve grup üyeliğinizi de kaldırır.

Uygulamaya artık erişemiyorsanız “Motoseyir veri silme” konulu bir e-postayı erengn@gmail.com adresine gönderin; varsa kullanıcı adınızı yazın. İsterseniz gönderdiğiniz otel ve tamirci önerilerini de sileriz. Verileriniz 30 gün içinde silinir ve size haber verilir. Satın alma kayıtları Google Play ya da App Store'da durur, biz silemeyiz.
